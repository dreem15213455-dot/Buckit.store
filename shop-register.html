Franchise Partner Terms

1. Franchise Partnership

By registering as a Franchise Partner on ZyMart+, you agree to operate as an authorized local partner in your designated area. You will provide accurate information about your business including valid ID proof, correct address, and genuine contact details.

2. Investment & Fees

The franchise registration fee is non-refundable. GST at applicable rates (currently 18%) is charged additionally. The fee covers platform access, franchise onboarding, and shop visibility on ZyMart+ for the agreed period.

3. Product Listings

As a Franchise Partner, you agree to list only genuine products with accurate descriptions, prices, and stock availability. Fake, misleading, or prohibited items will result in immediate termination of franchise agreement.

4. Order Fulfillment

You must fulfill confirmed orders within the promised time window. Repeated cancellations, delays, or non-fulfillment may lead to temporary or permanent franchise suspension.

5. Customer Service

As an authorized partner, maintain the highest standards of professionalism. Any harassment, fraud, or rude behavior reported by customers will result in permanent termination with no refund.

6. Platform Commission

ZyMart+ charges a commission on each order as communicated separately. Commission rates are subject to change with prior notice. Current rates will be shared via WhatsApp/email after onboarding.

7. Data & Privacy

Your business data will be used to operate the platform and connect you with customers. We do not share your data with third parties without consent, except as required by Indian law.

8. Compliance

You must comply with all applicable Indian laws including GST, FSSAI (if selling food), Shop & Establishment Act, and local municipal regulations. You are responsible for maintaining valid licenses.

9. Termination

ZyMart+ reserves the right to suspend or terminate any franchise without prior notice for policy violations, complaints, or suspicious activity. No refund will be provided in such cases.

10. Disputes

Any disputes arising from this agreement will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Maharashtra, India.

11. Updates

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

By clicking "I Accept", you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to these Franchise Partner Terms & Conditions.
