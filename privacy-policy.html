Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zymart ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

✅ Your Trust Matters: We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is used solely to provide you the best shopping experience.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Creation: Name, Email, Phone Number
  • Order Placement: Delivery Address, Payment Details, GPS Location
  • Shop Registration: Business Name, Owner Details, Shop Location (GPS Verified)
  • Automatically Collected: Device Info, IP Address, App Usage Data
  • Customer Support: Chat History, Support Tickets

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order updates and promotional offers
  • Improve our app and services
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in these situations:

  • With Shop Owners: To fulfill your order
  • With Delivery Partners: To deliver your order
  • With Payment Processors: For secure payments (Razorpay)
  • When Required by Law: To comply with legal requests

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Payments processed through PCI-compliant gateways
  • Regular security reviews and audits

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request account and data deletion
  • Opt-out of promotional communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, data is removed within 30 days, except as required by law.
