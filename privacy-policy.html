ZyMart Privacy

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how ZyMart collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you create an account or place an order.

Location Data: GPS coordinates to provide accurate delivery and show nearby shops.

Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, and order history to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

• To process and deliver your orders

• To send order confirmations and delivery updates

• To show relevant products and shops near you

• To improve our app and customer service

• To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We share your delivery address and phone number with shops only to fulfill your orders. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure servers to protect your data. Payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay.

5. Location Data

GPS location is collected only when you choose to share it. You can browse products without sharing your location. Location data helps us show nearby shops and calculate accurate delivery.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for app functionality. No tracking cookies are used for advertising purposes.

7. Your Rights

• Access and update your personal information

• Request deletion of your account and data

• Opt out of promotional communications
