Privacy Policy
Last Updated: April 21, 2026
At Zatpat, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app and services.
Your Trust Matters: We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is only used to provide you with the best shopping experience.
1. Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us when you:
  • Create an account (name, email, phone number)
  • Place an order (delivery address, payment details)
  • Contact customer support
  • Register your shop (business details, location)
We also automatically collect certain information when you use our app, including:
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Location data (for delivery purposes)
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent)
2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate order updates and promotional offers
  • Improve our app and services
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security
  • Comply with legal obligations
3. Information Sharing
We share your information only in the following circumstances:
  • With Shop Owners: To fulfill your order (name, address, phone)
  • With Delivery Partners: To deliver your order
  • With Payment Processors: To process payments securely
  • When Required by Law: To comply with legal requests
We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:
  • All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS
  • Payment information is processed through secure PCI-compliant gateways (Razorpay)
  • We regularly review our security practices
5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of promotional communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time
You can manage your data through the Profile section in the app or by contacting us directly.
6. Cookies & Tracking
We use cookies and similar technologies to:
  • Remember your preferences
  • Keep you logged in
  • Analyze app usage for improvements
7. Children's Privacy
Zatpat is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.
8. Data Retention
We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where required by law.
9. International Data Transfers
Your information may be transferred and processed in India where our servers are located. By using Zatpat, you consent to this transfer.
10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
