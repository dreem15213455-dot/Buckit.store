Refund & Cancellation Policy
Last Updated: April 21, 2026
At Zatpat, customer satisfaction is our priority. This policy explains our refund and cancellation procedures.
Quick Summary: Cancel within 2 minutes for full refund. Report issues within 1 hour for refund/replacement.
1. Order Cancellation
By Customer:
  • Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placement for a full refund
  • After 2 minutes, cancellation may not be possible as preparation begins
  • To cancel, go to My Orders and click "Cancel Order"
By Shop/Zatpat:
  • We reserve the right to cancel orders due to unavailability, pricing errors, or suspected fraud
  • Full refund will be issued for orders cancelled by us
2. Refund Eligibility
Refunds are issued in the following cases:
ReasonRefund Amount
Order cancelled within 2 minutes100% refund
Order cancelled by shop/Zatpat100% refund
Missing itemsPro-rated refund
Wrong item deliveredFull refund or replacement
Poor quality/spoiledFull refund or replacement
Delivery delayed > 60 minutesDelivery fee refund
3. Reporting Issues
  • Report any issues within 1 hour of delivery
  • Provide clear photos of the issue (missing items, damaged products)
  • Reports can be made through My Orders or WhatsApp Support
4. Refund Process
  • Prepaid Orders: Refund to original payment method within 5-7 business days
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund to Zatpat Wallet or bank account (within 3-5 days)
  • You'll receive email/SMS confirmation once refund is initiated
5. Shop Registration Fee
  • Registration fee is non-refundable once payment is made
  • If shop registration is rejected by admin, fee will be refunded
6. Non-Refundable Items
The following are generally non-refundable:
  • Perishable items (fruits, vegetables, dairy) unless spoiled/damaged
  • Personal care items (for hygiene reasons)
  • Customized or special order items
7. Delivery Issues
  • If no one is available to receive delivery, order may be cancelled without refund
  • Incorrect address provided by customer: no refund
  • Delivery delayed due to unforeseen circumstances (weather, traffic): no refund unless > 60 min late
8. How to Request Refund
Option 1: In App
Go to My Orders → Select Order → Report Issue → Follow prompts
Option 2: WhatsApp Support
Send message with Order ID and issue details to our WhatsApp number.
