Refund Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

⚠️ Important: Refunds are processed only for eligible cancellations. Please read the Cancellation Policy before requesting a refund.

1. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a refund in the following cases:

  • Order cancelled before the shop accepts it — 100% refund
  • Order cancelled by Zymart due to stock unavailability — 100% refund
  • Wrong or damaged product delivered — 100% refund or replacement
  • Delivery not made within promised time — Delivery fee refund
  • Duplicate payment or technical error — Full refund of excess amount

2. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Order cancelled after delivery has started
  • Perishable items (fruits, vegetables, dairy, etc.) once delivered
  • Change of mind after order delivery
  • Incorrect address provided by customer
  • Customer not available at delivery location

3. Refund Method

  • UPI/Online Payment: Refunded to original payment source within 3-5 business days
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund via UPI/Bank Transfer within 24-48 hours
  • Wallet Credit: Instant credit to Zymart Wallet

4. Refund Timeline

  • Refund initiated within 24 hours of cancellation approval
  • UPI/Bank: 3-5 business days
  • Wallet: Instant
