Refund & Cancellation Policy
Last Updated: April 2026
1. Order Cancellation
You can cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it. To cancel, go to My Orders > select order > click Cancel. Orders already prepared or out for delivery cannot be cancelled.
2. Refund Eligibility
Refunds are eligible in the following cases: Wrong item delivered, Damaged product, Expired product, Missing items. Refund requests must be made within 24 hours of delivery.
3. Refund Process
Once approved, refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days. For UPI payments, amount will be credited to the same UPI ID used for payment.
4. Non-Refundable Items
Perishable items (fruits, vegetables, dairy) cannot be refunded unless damaged/expired. Customized/personalized items are non-refundable.
